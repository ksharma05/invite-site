# Project Rules & Content Guidelines

## Kashi / Varanasi Content Boundary Rule

- **Visual Reference Only:** Kashi / Varanasi serves strictly as an aesthetic, visual, and atmospheric motif (cinematic background videos, temple arches, ghat silhouettes, Ganga aarti imagery).
- **No Event Relevance for Guests:** There is NO event taking place in Kashi and NO geographic relevance of Kashi for the guests.
- **Strict Prohibition on Location / Destination Framing:**
  - **Never** state or imply that the wedding is taking place in Kashi or Varanasi (e.g., "celebrate our wedding in Varanasi (Kashi)", "getting married in Kashi", "in Kashi").
  - **Never** use location-based titles or subtitles like "The Sacred Ghats of Kashi", "काशी की पावन धरा", "पावन नगरी काशी", "Welcoming You to the Eternal City of Kashi", "काशी विश्वनाथ की पावन नगरी में आपका स्वागत है", or "Sacred Memories in Kashi".
  - Keep all guest-facing titles, subtitles, card copy, and metadata focused purely on the wedding celebration and the sacred union of Sanjukta & Khushal.
